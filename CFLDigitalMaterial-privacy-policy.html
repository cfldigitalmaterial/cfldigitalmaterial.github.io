Privacy Policy

CFLDigitalMaterial · effective 19 September 2026

This policy covers two things: the educational files we sell, and the private software tool we use to prepare and publish our own marketing (“CFL Pin Studio”). It is written to describe what actually happens, not to reserve rights we do not exercise.

The short version. We do not collect personal information from the people who use our worksheets, we do not run analytics or trackers in the files we sell, and our publishing tool touches only our own Pinterest account. We have no user accounts, no mailing list operated from this site, and nothing to sell to anyone else.

1. The products we sell

  • Our worksheets, study guides and answer keys are self-contained files that run entirely on the buyer’s own device. Answers typed into a practice page are checked by code inside that page.
  • Nothing a student types is transmitted anywhere. The files contain no analytics, no trackers, no advertising, no external network requests, and no account or sign-in of any kind. They work with the internet switched off.
  • Progress is not stored on any server. Where a page remembers answers between sessions, it does so only in the browser’s own local storage on that device, and clearing the browser removes it.
  • Purchases are handled by the marketplace you bought from (for example Etsy). Those companies process your order and payment details under their own privacy policies; we receive only what the marketplace shows a seller, such as an order record and any message you send us.

2. CFL Pin Studio (our publishing tool)

CFL Pin Studio is software that runs locally on the shop owner’s own computer. It is not a website, not a service offered to the public, and has no users other than the shop owner. It exists to prepare our own images and text and publish them to our own Pinterest boards.

  • Who it acts for: only the shop owner’s own Pinterest business account, authorised by that person through Pinterest’s standard OAuth consent screen.
  • What it accesses: the account’s board names and identifiers, and permission to create pins on those boards. It does not read followers, messages, other people’s content, or any personal data of Pinterest users.
  • What it stores, and where: an access token and refresh token, written to a permission-restricted file on that one computer. Nothing is uploaded to us, because there is no “us” to upload to — there is no server, no database and no cloud component.
  • What it never does: collect data about Pinterest users, scrape Pinterest, act on behalf of anyone else’s account, or share, sell or transfer any data to third parties.
  • Revoking access: the account owner can disconnect the app at any time from Pinterest account settings, which immediately invalidates the stored tokens.

3. This website

This page is a static document. It sets no cookies, runs no scripts, and collects no information about visitors. Whoever hosts the page may keep ordinary server logs under their own policy.

4. Children

Our materials are designed to be used by students, including children, but they are bought by adults and they collect nothing. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age. Because the files transmit nothing, a child using a worksheet leaves no data with us at all.

5. Your choices

  • Because we hold no accounts and no profiles, there is generally nothing for us to look up, correct or delete.
  • If you have messaged us through a marketplace, that correspondence lives in the marketplace’s system, and you can ask us to delete our copy of it.
  • To ask a question about this policy, message us through our shop page on the marketplace where you bought — that reaches us directly.

6. Changes

If our practices change — for example if we ever add a mailing list — we will update this page and change the effective date above. We will not quietly begin collecting something this page says we do not collect.
